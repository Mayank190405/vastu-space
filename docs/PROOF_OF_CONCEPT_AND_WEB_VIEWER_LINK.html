4. Free-Roaming Navigation & Control Guide

A. Desktop Keyboard & Mouse (First-Person Roaming)

  • W / ↑: Move forward along camera gaze
  • S / ↓: Move backward
  • A / ←: Strafe left | D / →: Strafe right
  • Mouse Look: 360° pitch and yaw look (Click the canvas to lock cursor, press Esc to release)
  • Shift: Sprint / Accelerate walking speed
  • Double-Click: Place 3D distance ruler to measure Euclidean clearances in meters (±0.01m accuracy)

B. Mobile & Tablet Touch Controls

  • Left Screen Area: Virtual floating joystick for omnidirectional walking
  • Right Screen Area: Touch drag to pan camera look angle

C. Built-in Physics & Layer Controls

  • Floor Clamping: Camera height is constrained at eye level (1.60m) above the horizontal floor datum.
  • Collision Boundaries: An elliptical collider (radius = 0.35m) prevents camera penetration through physical walls and furniture.
  • Toolbar Toggles: Toggle Walls, Furniture Obstacles, 3D NavMesh route, Vāstu Directional POIs, and Wireframe Mode.
